Much like the Palouse Choral Society, the PCS Oral History Collection is made possible by community involvement! The collection will consist of interviews, programs, photographs, and other items that help tell the story of this community choir.

If you are interested in contributing some items to the project, please review our Curation Guidelines first. While we would love to include everything our community has to offer, time, labor, and space constraints limit how much we can realistically process and maintain. 

Please be prepared to provide some information about each item you submit to us, as this will help streamline the process of adding it to the collection. We are able to accept both physical and digital items:
- Any physical items will be digitized with the help of the University of Idaho Library and returned to you as soon as possible. Please contact someone from the project team to arrange this.
- For digital items, follow the link below to fill out our contribution form.
