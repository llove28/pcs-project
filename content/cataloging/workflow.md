Review the following steps before collecting/cataloging items for the PCS Oral History Project.

{% capture text %}Tools you will need:

- **Contributor Agreement:** Contributors must sign this document giving us permission to publish their contributed items.
- **Metadata & File Naming System:** A guide for naming and collecting descriptive information about digital item files.
- **Metadata Checklist:** Print and use in the event that you are collecting physical items/interviews and do not have ready access to the Contributor Form. Make sure to use the Form or add this information to the PCS spreadsheet manually later.
- **Contributor Form:** A Google form for accepting items and logging related metadata. 
- **PCS Spreadsheet:** Answers from the Contributor Form will appear here. Our team can also manually add items to this list or make edits as necessary. This spreadsheet eventually be used to generate the collection on the website.
- **Collection Folders:** Where you will find and sort all of the items
- **Curation Guidelines:** A guide for selecting which items will be added to the collection.{% endcapture %} {% include alert.html text=text color=secondary %}
