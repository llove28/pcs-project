Welcome to the PCS Oral History Project!

This website was designed to guide us through the development of the Palouse Choral Society Oral History Collection. The project was initiated in Spring 2026, with completion currently set for Fall 2027. 

- **Phase 1:** Set up (Spring 2026)
- **Phase 2:** Trial/Proof of Concept (Summer 2026)
- **Phase 3:** Development (Summer/Fall 2026 & Spring 2027)
- **Phase 4:** QC (Spring/Summer 2027)
- **Phase 5:** Launch (Fall 2027)
